1. Executive Summary & Scientific Mission

While your Midterm Research Project dissected the multi-omic causes of human disease (Weeks 1–7: germline variants, structural CNVs, GWAS/PRS, chromatin conformation, and GTEx eQTLs), the second half of Genomics in Medicine (Weeks 8–14) confronts the translational gauntlet: How do we turn a molecular target into an effective therapy that survives randomized clinical trials and safely navigates human pharmacogenomic diversity?

Your Mission: Select one precision therapeutic regimen, targeted drug, biologic/cell therapy, or pharmacogenomic intervention—representing a brand-new disease or drug distinct from your Midterm topic. You will author a publication-grade End-to-End Translational & Therapeutic Dossier auditing your therapy across five empirical databases:

  1. Target & Somatic/Structural Landscape: cBioPortal, COSMIC, RCSB PDB / AlphaFold, or ClinVar.
  2. Drug Engineering, Delivery Route & Packaging: Small molecule (-ib), antibody/ADC (-mab), CAR-T (-cel), ASO/siRNA, or CRISPR; oral first-pass vs. parenteral delivery.
  3. Clinical Trial Forensics: ClinicalTrials.gov NCT record, control arm fairness, Intention-to-Treat vs. Per-Protocol, Hazard Ratio, Number Needed to Treat (NNT), surrogate paradox, and enrollment diversity.
  4. Pharmacogenomic Safety Gate: ClinPGx (PharmGKB) & CPIC Level 1A dosing guidelines, Star-Allele Activity Score math, prodrug vs. active-drug risk inversion, and gnomAD ancestry frequencies.
  5. Bioethics, Cost-Effectiveness & AI Stress-Test: Wholesale cost, $/QALY, press-release hype vs. FDA Boxed Warnings, and stress-testing a frontier LLM on a complex patient edge case.
⚠️ Two Essential Ground Rules: Fresh Topic & The Regimen-Wide PGx Rule
  • 1. Mandatory Non-Overlap with Midterm: You must choose a brand-new disease, drug, or therapeutic modality distinct from your Midterm Research Project. Recycling narrative text or figures from your Midterm is strictly prohibited.
  • 2. The Regimen-Wide Pharmacogenomics (PGx) Rule for Biologics / CAR-T / Gene Therapies: If your primary drug is a small molecule with a direct CPIC / FDA pharmacogene (e.g., Clopidogrel/CYP2C19, Warfarin/CYP2C9+VKORC1, Codeine/CYP2D6, Tamoxifen/CYP2D6, Abacavir/HLA-B*57:01, Simvastatin/SLCO1B1), audit that primary gene–drug pair. If your primary therapy is a biologic (-mab), ADC, CAR-T (-cel), ASO, or CRISPR therapy not cleared by CYP450s, you must audit the mandatory CPIC / ClinPGx pharmacogene governing its combination chemotherapy backbone, conditioning regimen, or toxicity-rescue drug (e.g., DPYD & UGT1A1 in FOLFIRINOX/capecitabine backbones; G6PD rasburicase in CAR-T/leukemia tumor lysis prophylaxis; TPMT + NUDT15 in ALL maintenance; APOE ε4/ε4 ARIA gating in Alzheimer's mAbs; or busulfan GSTP1 conditioning in Casgevy).

2. The Two-Track Generative AI Contract

Declare your authoring track on your title page. All submissions pass through automated PubMed PMID verification, GRCh38/NCT/CPIC identifier validation, and synthetic boilerplate screening:

Track A

AI-Assisted Synthesis & Adversarial Audit

  • Evaluative Standard: Graded against Expert Peer Reviewer Standards (NEJM / Lancet Oncology rigor). Generic AI filler is penalized ruthlessly.
  • Mandatory Table 2 (AI Audit & Clinical Stress-Test Table): Must document ≥3 specific instances where an LLM hallucinated a trial statistic, star-allele score, coordinate, or unsafe clinical dosing recommendation, with primary literature refutations.
  • Zero-Tolerance Citation Policy: Every claim requires a verified PMID/DOI. Any fabricated citation receives an automatic zero on the bibliography and a 1-letter grade penalty.
Track B

100% Human-Authored

  • Evaluative Standard: Graded on upper-division undergraduate criteria for conceptual synthesis, accurate trial/PGx analysis, and authentic scientific voice.
  • Tier 8 AI Vignette Permitted: Track B authors still run the single Tier 8B clinical prompt on an LLM to critique its diagnostic output, while writing 100% of their paper prose themselves.
  • Integrity Guardrail: Undeclared AI prose or fabricated citations in Track B are treated as academic integrity violations (automatic zero).

4. The Translational Characterization Tiers (Tiers 1–8 + Tier 9 for Honors/5111)

Structure your manuscript into clearly titled sections corresponding to each tier:

Tier 1: Disease Pathophysiology & The Unmet Therapeutic Bottleneck Clinical Baseline

Define the disease, anatomical/cellular site, staging, and why historical standard-of-care therapy (e.g., non-selective cytotoxic chemotherapy, empirical trial-and-error dosing) fails or produces unacceptable off-target organ toxicity.

Tier 2: Molecular Target Architecture & Somatic / Immunogenomic Landscape cBioPortal • COSMIC • PDB

Identify the exact molecular target (HGNC symbol, Ensembl ID, GRCh38 coordinates, protein structural domains). For oncology topics, query cBioPortal / COSMIC to report somatic mutation frequencies, hotspot residues (e.g., KRAS p.Gly12Cys, EGFR p.Leu858Arg), or mutational signatures. For non-oncology topics, report ClinVar alleles, HLA binding-groove pockets, or target expression.

Tier 3: Precision Therapeutic Engineering, Delivery Route & Packaging Drug Design • First-Pass PK

Classify the modality (-ib kinase/allosteric inhibitor, -mab antibody/ADC, -cel CAR-T, gapmer ASO, siRNA, or CRISPR). Explain why its specific route of administration (oral pill vs. IV vs. intrathecal) and packaging/formulation (prodrug mask, enteric coating, cleavable ADC linker, LNP, AAV capsid) are required, and how hepatic first-pass metabolism shapes bioavailability.

Tier 4: Pivotal Clinical Trial Architecture & Endpoint Forensics ClinicalTrials.gov • Surrogates

Audit the pivotal trial on ClinicalTrials.gov (exact NCT number, phase, sample size N, biomarker inclusion gate, randomization, and blinding). Critique control arm fairness, Intention-to-Treat vs. Per-Protocol analysis, and whether the primary endpoint is a true clinical outcome (Overall Survival) or a surrogate (PFS, ORR, biomarker)—evaluating the Surrogate Paradox.

Tier 5: Biostatistical Efficacy, NNT & Trial Demographic Representation HR / OR • NNT • Equity

Report the primary statistical outcomes: Hazard Ratio (HR) or Odds Ratio (OR), 95% CI, p-value, Absolute Risk Reduction (ARR), and calculate Number Needed to Treat (NNT = 1 / ARR). Audit the trial's baseline demographic table (% Black, Hispanic, Asian, White) against real-world disease epidemiology. (Honors/5111: Perform a head-to-head comparative autopsy of TWO clinical trials).

Tier 6: Pharmacogenomic ADME & Toxicity Safety Gate (ClinPGx / CPIC) ClinPGx • CPIC • Star Alleles

Apply the Regimen-Wide PGx Rule using ClinPGx (PharmGKB) and CPIC Guidelines. Report the key star alleles (*2, *3, *5, *17, *28) or rsIDs, calculate the CPIC Activity Score (AS) across metabolizer phenotypes, explain active-drug vs. prodrug risk inversion, evaluate drug–drug phenoconversion, and compare allele frequencies across global ancestries in gnomAD.

Tier 7: Bioethics, Pharmacoeconomics, Health Policy & Access Equity $/QALY • GINA • Policy

Report the Wholesale Acquisition Cost (WAC) per year/course and evaluate cost-effectiveness (ICER in $/QALY relative to $100k–$150k/QALY). Examine insurance prior-authorization barriers, GINA statutory protections vs. life/disability insurance loopholes, or DTC testing pitfalls.

Tier 8: The Hype vs. Reality Verdict & Clinical AI Stress-Test FDA Label vs. PR • LLM Audit

Part A (Hype Audit): Contrast a manufacturer press release or DTC ad against the actual FDA Boxed Warnings and Overall Survival data.
Part B (AI Stress-Test): Prompt a frontier LLM (ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini) with a complex clinical vignette combining your drug/regimen with a high-risk CPIC diplotype (or unphased cis/trans / phenoconversion trap). Critique what the LLM missed and write the verified clinical consult note.

Tier 9 (Honors / BIOL 5111 Requirement): Acquired Resistance & Next-Gen Countermeasures Honors • BIOL 5111

Model the Darwinian mechanism of acquired treatment failure (e.g., on-target gatekeeper mutation like EGFR C797S or KRAS Y96D, BRCA2 frame-restoring reversion indels, CD19 splice-skipping antigen loss, or pathway bypass) and evaluate a next-generation therapeutic countermeasure.
